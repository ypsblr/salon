FIP MEMBER ID : LM 013007

Santosh Kumar Jana (Born 1973) in the Midnapur district of West Bengal. He passed B.Sc. examination from the University of Calcutta and got a diploma on Photography from Bangiya Bijnan Parishad. Some of his achievements are: 
•Three successive Awards in NATIONAL PHOTO CONTEST (Ministry of Information & Broadcasting-Govt. of India) 2nd, 3rd & 2nd (2010, 2011,2012)
• Better Photography Magazine – Won 1st & 2nd Awards in Action Photographer of The Year (2000, 2002) 
• Won the 1st Award (3 LAKHS CASH) at D.J.M.P.C Photo Contest
• Won Award in Commonwealth Photo Contest (LONDON)
• Won 1st Award at All India Fine Arts & Craft Society, Govt. of India. 
• Sanctuary Asia Award 2 times
• Won more than 750 Awards in National AND International Salons

He is deeply involved in photographic activities - As he has been the Salon chairman of Eight international Salons and Seven Circuits. He is a life member of the FIP since 2000.
