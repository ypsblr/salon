FIP Member ID : LM 1023

Nagendra Muthmurdu, born and residing in a small village near Sirsi of Uttara Kannada District in the Western Ghats region of Karnataka, India, is an agriculturist by occupation. After graduating with a B.Sc., he pursued playing classical music on the flute and has given numerous stage performances. 

Later, he turned to photography as an art form, which became his passion. Pictorial photography being his main genre, he works on Nature, Travel, Birds, Macro, and Street photography, too.

He has secured EFIAP & FFIP distinctions and has to his credit 150+ awards, which include 30+ gold medals (amongst them 18 International) and 1800+ acceptances in international and national photography salons. His pictures have been exhibited in more than 30 countries. He is a winner of 'FIAP Best author' and 'Best Indian entrant' awards multiple times in international photography salons. He has judged numerous National & International salons of photography. He has held many one-man exhibitions & webinars on photography art and has been a mentor and faculty in several photography workshops and training camps. 

Considering his contribution to the art of photography, he has been honoured and felicitated in Manama, Bahrain, with the 'SAARTHA Award-2010' for photography. A 40-minute interview on his journey in Photography has been telecast in 'Belagu’ program of ‘Dooradarshana Chandana’ Kannada Channel.

Being a writer, many of his articles and photos have been published in several leading Kannada magazines, periodicals, special issues and cover pages. He has served as a Guest lecturer at RV University, Bengaluru. Education being his loving field, he is serving as Vice Secretary in the Managing Committee -Sri Kalika Bhavani Secondary Education Society, Kanasuru, Siddapura (U.K.), Karnataka. Trekking, music, reading and writing are his other hobbies.
