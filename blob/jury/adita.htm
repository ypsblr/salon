FIP MEMBER ID : LM-735

Adit Agarwala (Born 1950), an Alumni of Photographic Association of Dumdum, Kolkata (PAD) served as Honorary Lecture of Photography at PAD from 1983 - 1998. He is the co-author of two Books “Learn Photography” and “Experimental Photography” published by PAD. He has delivered Lectures on Art of Photography to many Photographic Clubs. He also has served as a member of the Organizing Committee of several International Photographic Conferences organized by PAD from 1983 - 1998.

Has been awarded Photographic Distinctions FRPS, EFIAP/d1, FAPU, EFIP, and also bestowed with some ‘Service to Photography oriented Honours’ – Hon. FIP, Hon. YPS, Hon. EFMPA, GPA.PESGSPC, Hon. GMTPAS, F.DCC.

He has been acting as a Member of Jury in National & International Salons since 1987. He is the Founder President of ‘Friends of Photography, Chandigarh’ from 1999 – 2004. Has been Advisor to Tri-City Photo Art Society of Chandigarh from 2015-2019.

He has been Associate Editor of VIEWFINDER - the FIP House Journal from 1998-2002. Has been a member of ‘Editorial Team’ of BLUE SAPPHIRE – a Coffee Table Book published by the Federation of Indian Photography (FIP). He has served the FIP in various capacity as a Member of its Working Committee, as Vice President and then as the President from 2021 till 2024. He is presently serving an Ex-Officio Immediate Past President of FIP.
