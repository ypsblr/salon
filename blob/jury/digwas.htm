Born in the serene town of Sirsi, nestled amidst the lush green Western Ghats of Karnataka, India, Digwas Bellemane is a self-taught photo artist whose passion for photography began in 2008. 

Currently based in Bengaluru and self-employed in the field of electronics, Digwas has transformed his innate curiosity about the visual world into an art form that transcends boundaries. In the early stages of his journey, Digwas worked with conventional methods of photography, focusing on traditional compositions and techniques. Over time, his creative vision evolved, fueled by the motivation to explore new approaches that challenged the norms of visual storytelling. This transformation led him to develop a distinctive style, defined by layered narratives, emotional depth, and timeless appeal.

A Journey of Excellence
Since 2008, Digwas has grown from a passionate beginner to a globally recognized photo artist. Over the years, he has participated in countless international exhibitions and salons, earning widespread acclaim for his unique ability to capture raw, fleeting moments that evoke emotion and inspire interpretation. His work has received recognition from some of the most esteemed organizations in the photographic world.
