Satish started photography at a young age of 12, but took it up seriously from 1981 onwards. He has 44 years of experience in the field of Art of Photography and is the first Indian to be honoured with EFIAP and MFIAP in Nature colour prints, and the youngest Indian to be awarded the MFIAP. He is the first Indian to get two ‘Master of Photography’ from world bodies of photography in Nature photography. 

He has garnered 1375+ awards, among 5600+ pictures exhibited in numerous national and international salons/exhibitions. He has given 47 Webinars from 2020 to date, on various subjects of Photography. 

Many of his pictures have been published in international magazines, catalogs, newspapers, brochures, and books. His pictures have graced all the guidebooks brought out by 'Outlook Traveller' and 'Today- Travel Plus' magazines, and calendar spreads of WWF, BEML, BHEL, MICO, MCF, Canara Bank and many more.

Andhra Pradesh Photography Academy has published a book on Satish's journey of wildlife photography. He is the recipient of Karnataka’s ‘State Artist’- an award given for his achievements in the field of Art of Photography by Karnataka Lalitkala Academy. 

He served as the Secretary, Vice President and President of Youth Photographic Society, which is India's No.1 photography club.  He has been a member of the club for 45 years and served as its President for 7 years.
